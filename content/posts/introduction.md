# Introduction

Hullo!  I'm Chris, a software engineer making his living in the scenic Pacific
Northwest.  Recently, I made the decision to change both my phone number as well
as to abandon the [GMail](https://gmail.com) account I've had for over 20 years
and replace it with one that isn't plastered all over the dark web.  Since this
is a *big* undertaking, I've created this blog to document how I went about
changing everything as well as any pitfalls I've run into in the hopes that this
information will be useful to others.

A few things to be aware of:

1. As of this writing, I have not procured a new phone number yet as I do not
   plan to get a new number until the end of December 2025.
2. While I've created a couple of new GMail accounts, they are not considered
   "active" yet.  This will happen once I get a new phone number.
3. Do not expect much content here until after I get a new cell phone number
   since that is when I will start migrating various services.
4. I will be writing about the why's and wherefore's of me making this change in
   future posts.  Be patient.
   
So feel free to bookmark this site, grab your favorite caffeinated beverage, and
watch out for new posts.
